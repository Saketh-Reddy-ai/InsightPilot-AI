How the split is calculated

The overall return rate is a weighted average: each segment's own rate, weighted by its share of items. So a change in the total can only come from two places — the shares moved, or the rates moved. The split is exact and leaves no residual:

Δ rate = Σ (share_now − share_before) × rate_before ← mix + Σ share_now × (rate_now − rate_before) ← rate

Mix is what you get if every segment had held its old return rate and only the traffic composition changed. Rate is what you get if the composition had held still and only behaviour changed.

The split depends on what you segment by. A shift that is invisible inside one dimension shows up clearly in another, which is why the control at the top changes the answer and not just the chart.

Counts come from one weekly grain query over bigquery-public-data.thelook_ecommerce — week × traffic source × category, returned items and total items. Every rate on this page is derived from those counts at display time, so filtering never averages an average. Cancelled items are excluded, because an item that never shipped could never be returned.
